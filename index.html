I am a 3rd year PhD student in CSE department at IIT Delhi, advised by Prof. Mausam and Prof. Parag Singla . I do research on Natural Language Processing. This includes studying the behaviour of multilingual pre-trained models for relation extraction in distantly supervised setting. Recently, I am targeting to study this in the context of Indic Languages as well.

Before joining IIT Delhi, I received my Masters from IISc Bangalore, where I started my journey in Machine Learning and NLP research.
