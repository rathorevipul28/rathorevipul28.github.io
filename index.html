I am a PhD student in CSE department at IIT Delhi, advised by Prof. Mausam and Prof. Parag Singla . I am working upon Multilingual Natural Language Processing (NLP). This includes prompting pre-trained multilingual models for relation extraction in distantly supervised setting. Currently, I am building techniques for Indic Languages, specially in Zero-Shot Cross-Lingual settings.

Before joining IIT Delhi, I received my Masters from IISc Bangalore, where I started my journey in ML and NLP research while working under Dr. Partha Talukdar on Procedural Information Extraction. I was also fortunate to received my Bachelors degree from IIT Bombay in EE Department.
