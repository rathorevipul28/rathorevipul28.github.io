I am on the job market and open to research positions in NLP/AI, available from October 2026.


I am a PhD graduate from CSE department at IIT Delhi, advised by Prof. Mausam and Prof. Parag Singla . My thesis focuses on building hybrid models for cross-lingual transfer to low-resource languages for structured prediction problems. Currently, I am exploring multilingual agents, cross-cultural adaptation of LLMs and multimodal extension of my hybrid NLP methods for Audio models and Visual LLMs (VLMs).

Before joining IIT Delhi, I received my Masters from IISc Bangalore, where I started my journey in ML and NLP research while working under Dr. Partha Talukdar on Procedural Information Extraction. I was also fortunate to receive my Bachelors degree from IIT Bombay in EE Department.
